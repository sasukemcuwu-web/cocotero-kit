# Plantilla de prompt para una toma de video (generar_video)

Escríbelo en inglés (el estudio responde mejor), llénalo y no agregues más de una acción.

```
Vertical 9:16, <4|6|8|10> seconds, one single continuous shot, no cuts, no transitions, handheld phone video,
soft natural window light, clean bright bathroom / vanity with green plants.
<Who>: a <age> Latina woman with <hair> and fresh natural skin.
<Product>: <exact description from the supplier photo: color, shape, material, size>.
<Action>: she <one single action with the product>, <where on the face/hands>.
Camera: <close-up | medium shot>, steady, product clearly visible in the first second.
No text, no captions, no logos, no music.
```

Ejemplo (masajeador capilar, toma B "uso"):
```
Vertical 9:16, 6 seconds, one single continuous shot, no cuts, no transitions, handheld phone video, soft natural
window light, clean bright bathroom with green plants. A 27-year-old Latina woman with long dark wavy hair in the shower,
shampoo foam in her hair. She gently massages her scalp with a round mint-green silicone scalp massager with soft
bristles. Medium close-up, steady, the massager clearly visible in the first second. No text, no captions, no logos,
no music.
```
